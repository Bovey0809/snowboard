# Example frame attribution

All example images are derived from freely-licensed footage. They are analysis
overlays, so the underlying frames remain under the original licence.

- `overlay_frame.jpg`, `hud.jpg`, `edge_trace.jpg`, `board_axis_check.jpg` — frames
  from *Highlights day 2 I Snowboard Cross Men and Women - Winter Universiade 2019*,
  by FISU / Krasnoyarsk 2019, via Wikimedia Commons, licensed **CC BY 3.0**.
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Highlights_day_2_I_Snowboard_Cross_Men_and_Women_-_Winter_Universiade_2019.webm

Other footage used during development is deliberately not shown here: the Mixkit
clips are covered by the Mixkit free licence, which does not permit redistributing
the items themselves.
